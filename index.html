Guide

How To Use ActuallyFreeTaxes.com

1. High-Level Features & Field Ratings (Yellow / Red / Green)

Every number on the page updates live as you type, showing both the general IRS/state formula and your exact numbers plugged in. Blue-bordered panels accept your inputs, while red-bordered panels highlight key outputs (like AGI and Total Tax).

!
Yellow Circles — Attention Radar: Your "how much should I care about this box?" meter. Pale yellow means safe to skip if it doesn't apply; brighter yellow means double-check it; and a glowing ! means don't miss this.
!
Red Circles — IRS Matching Heat: Shows how closely the IRS computer compares this line to W-2s and 1099s it already received. Soft pink is low-key; bold red with ! means the IRS already has a copy, so match it to the penny.
!
Green Circles — Money Savers: Spot the cash-back opportunities at a glance. White/empty means neutral reporting, while deeper green and ! flag deductions and credits that can shrink your tax bill fast.
Pro Tip: Use the toolbar filter chips (All Fields, Required Only, Money Savers, Dim Optional) to spotlight just the circles you care about, or click any rating pill for IRS context.

2. Step-by-Step: How To Use the Site

  1. Return Settings: Choose your Tax Year, Filing Status, and Primary Resident State (or load a Sample Scenario to explore).
  2. Section 1 (W-2s): Enter each W-2 (or remove all W-2s if you have none). Add multiple state/local lines on a W-2 if you moved or worked in NYC/Yonkers.
  3. Section 2 (Other Income, Adjustments & Credits): Click + Add Income Source for 1099-NEC, 1099-DIV, 1099-INT, 1099-R, SSA-1099, Schedule K-1, or W-2G. Optional $0 cards start collapsed and expand automatically when populated.
  4. Section 3 (Schedule A Itemized Deductions): Enter medical, property tax, mortgage interest, or charity; the engine automatically picks whichever is higher between Standard and Itemized (unless overridden).
  5. Sections 4–6 (Calculations & Final Summary): Review every Form 1040 line, bracket breakdown, and auto-generated resident/nonresident state returns. Hover any card to trace its Dependency Arrows.
  6. Drafts & Section 7 (Filing): Use the Drafts bar to save, switch, or copy drafts, and open Section 7 to print your 1040 packet, copy values into IRS Free File Fillable Forms, or submit via 1-Click e-File.

3. Fast Setup: Using an AI Agent to Generate & Upload Your Data

Important: This application does not run on AI, and makes no calls or ever sends any data to an AI. If you choose to use an external AI assistant (such as ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini) to convert your forms into JSON, redact your SSN, EIN, full legal name, street address, and account numbers before pasting anything into an AI chat.

Documents & details to attach or paste as comments to your AI:

4. Using an AI Agent to Double-Check Your Return

Once your numbers are loaded, you can ask an AI agent to sanity-check your return for omissions or mismatches. Documents & context to attach (redacted):

  • Your current return JSON (click Paste / Copy JSON → Copy JSON)
  • Redacted summaries of your W-2s, 1099s, Form 1098 (mortgage/property tax), and Schedule E rental records
  • Additional context in the prompt: tax year, filing status, states lived/worked in during the year, number of dependents, and any unusual life events (job switch, home purchase, move, retirement distribution)

Prompt to copy and give to your AI agent:

Please review my redacted tax return JSON and source document summaries below for potential errors, missing forms, or missed deductions/credits.

Additional Context:
- Tax Year: [e.g., 2024]
- Filing Status: [e.g., Single / Married Filing Jointly]
- States lived/worked in & dates if moved: [e.g., Lived & worked in NYC all year; own rental condo in DC]
- Dependents / special situations: [e.g., None / Switched employers mid-year]

Check specifically for:
1. Whether every W-2, 1099, and withholding amount matches my source documents.
2. Whether any state/local taxes, credits (OSTC, Child Tax Credit, EITC, AOTC), or caps (SALT $10k, SS wage cap, -$3k capital loss limit) look misapplied.
3. Any potential red flags or follow-up questions I should verify against IRS/state instructions.
